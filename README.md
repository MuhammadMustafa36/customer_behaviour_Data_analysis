# Retail Customer Behavior & Shopping Trends Analysis

## 📊 End-to-End Data Analytics Project

An end-to-end Data Analytics project analyzing retail customer behavior and shopping trends using **Python, SQL, PostgreSQL, and Power BI**.

The project follows a real-world analytics workflow, starting from raw customer transaction data and progressing through data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, SQL-based business analysis, and interactive dashboard development.

The main objective is to transform raw retail data into meaningful business insights that can help organizations understand their customers, sales performance, purchasing behavior, and product trends.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Retail companies generate large amounts of customer and transaction data, but raw data alone does not provide useful business insights.

The objective of this project is to answer important business questions such as:

- Who are the company's customers?
- How does purchasing behavior differ by gender?
- Which product categories generate the most revenue?
- Which products receive the most customer attention?
- How does customer purchasing behavior vary across different age groups?
- Which categories have the highest sales?
- What are the major customer shopping trends?
- How can data be used to support better business decisions?
